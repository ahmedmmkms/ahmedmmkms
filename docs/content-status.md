## Publishing discipline

Concept direction does not imply customer research or delivery. Proposed measurements do not imply results. Internal evidence and confidential material stay outside this public repository.

The catalogue follows the supplied portfolio master brief and uses GitHub-native Markdown with expandable detail and editorial illustrations.
